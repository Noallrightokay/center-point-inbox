What stays on your computer

RATA reads and sends your mail itself, over IMAP and SMTP. There is no RATA server between you and your mailbox, so there is nothing of yours on one to steal.

On your computer

  • Your mail passwordIn the system keychain: Keychain on a Mac, Credential Manager on Windows, Secret Service on Linux.
  • Your mailDownloaded from your mailbox and kept on disk, so it opens and searches without a connection.
  • Your signaturesNames, phone numbers and addresses are never copied to your online account.

On mailrata.org

  • Your account and licenceYour email address, your plan, and the key that unlocks the app.
  • BillingHandled by Stripe. RATA never sees your card.
  • Summaries and translations, only when you askThe text of that one message passes through to be summarized or translated, and is not stored or logged.

The licence is checked on your computer, so RATA opens and reads your mail even when you are offline.

A mail client, done properly

The things you reach for every day, working with any mailbox that speaks IMAP: Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, Fastmail or your own domain.

Every mailbox in one inbox

Link your mailboxes and read them in one list, with Sent, Drafts, Archive, Spam and your own folders alongside. On Pro, put them side by side when that is easier.

New mail as it arrives

RATA keeps a connection open to each inbox and shows a notification when something lands: the sender and subject, or only a count if you prefer.

Formatted mail, handled carefully

HTML mail is cleaned before it is shown, pictures from the web load only when you ask, and a link tells you where it goes before it opens.

Writing the way you expect

Reply all, Cc and Bcc, a signature for each address, attachments both ways, and drafts you started on your phone, ready to finish.

Files in the format people need

Turn an attachment or any document into Word, PDF, Markdown, Excel or CSV, on your computer, without uploading it anywhere.

Summaries and translation, when you ask

Summarize a long message or translate one in a click. RATA asks before the first time, and never sends anything on its own.
