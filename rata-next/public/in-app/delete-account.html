Delete your RATA account

Your RATA account is the one at mailrata.org that your licence comes from: the email address and password you sign in with there. You can delete it on this page.

What deleting it removes

  • your preferences and folder names
  • your subscription record
  • your AI usage record (only what your AI requests cost each month)
  • your login

Backups of the website’s database are encrypted before they leave the server, and each is deleted 90 days after it is made, so something deleted from the database is gone from every backup within 90 days.

What it cannot reach

  • your mail, which stays at your provider
  • your mailbox passwords, which are in your own computer’s keychain and never reached RATA
  • billing records, which the store or payment processor that took your payment must keep
  • what the RATA app holds on your device, until you uninstall it

If you pay for RATA through the App Store or Google Play, deleting your account does not cancel that subscription. Cancel it in the store first, or the store keeps charging you.

Deletion has to wait in a few cases, such as while a subscription is still live. If it does, this page says why. The privacy policy lists every case.

Everything listed under What deleting it removes is gone from mailrata.org, apart from the backups, which go within 90 days. Your mail is untouched at your provider, and the RATA app keeps what it holds on your device until you uninstall it.
