What RATA is

RATA is a desktop mail client for Windows, macOS and Linux. It connects to the mailboxes you already have, over IMAP and SMTP, and reads and sends your mail on your own computer. Your mailbox passwords stay in your computer’s keychain. mailrata.org sells the plans, issues the licence that unlocks the app, and runs the AI features you ask for.

Your mail stays with your mail provider, under that provider’s own terms. RATA is not made by, or connected with, any mail provider. When you delete, archive or move a message in RATA, it does that in your real mailbox, as you asked.

Your account

  • Pay with the same address as your account. The subscription and the licence are matched by that address, so a payment made with another one does not reach your account.

Your licence

Once your payment is confirmed, your licence page at mailrata.org shows a licence key. Paste it into RATA once. The app checks it on your computer, so RATA works offline.

  • Each key lasts 30 days. While your subscription is live, the app renews it by itself in its last week, whenever it is online.
  • When your subscription ends, the licence is no longer renewed. The key you hold keeps working until it expires, at most 30 days after it was issued.
  • A key that expired more than 90 days ago cannot renew itself. Sign in at mailrata.org to get a new one.
  • Without a current licence, RATA stops fetching and sending mail. What it has already downloaded stays on your computer.
  • The key is issued to you. Keep it to yourself: anyone with it can run RATA as you, and use your AI allowance, until it expires.

AI features

Translation is on Base and Pro; summaries and the briefing are on Pro. They run only when you press Translate, Summarize or Run briefing, and RATA asks before the first time. The text you send goes to Anthropic to be answered, as the privacy policy describes.

  • Each account has a monthly AI allowance. When it is used up, the AI features stop until the 1st of the next month, and RATA shows the quick version it makes on your computer instead.
  • AI answers can be wrong or leave things out. Check anything that matters against the message itself.
